# Interview guide

Two interviews per firm: mid-term (week 2, part 1) with the owner and one technician, final (week 4, parts 1 and 2) with the owner. 30–45 minutes, on site or by video. Notes go into the table at the end; findings into [findings.md](findings.md). Ask open questions first, then the numbers.

## Part 1 – Daily work (owner and technician)

**Report time (target: a report in under 5 minutes, MARA requirements).**
1. Walk me through yesterday's last job with the app, from arriving to leaving.
2. How long does a typical report take in the app, from first entry to signature? (Ask to time the next one, or look at the times: last entry to signature.)
3. What took longest? What did you skip or write somewhere else?
4. What did you do before the app (paper, WhatsApp, Excel)? How long did that take?

**Signatures on site (target: ≥ 80 % of reports signed on site).**
5. How often did the customer sign on the phone? When not, why not (absent, refused, no time)?
6. Did any customer ask about the PDF, the code or the data?
   - Measured alongside: share of SIGNED versus NOT_SIGNED reports per firm over the period (database, `report_signature.kind`).

**Offline and sync.**
7. Were you ever without reception while working? Did anything go missing or appear twice?
8. Did you see a message in the inbox (conflict, refused change)? Did you understand it?

**Office.**
9. Owner: how did invoicing from reports work? How long from a signed report to the invoice?
10. What would make you stop using the app?

## Part 2 – Pricing (owner, final interview)

Planned price: **€39 per month** for the firm (up to N technicians, to be decided), on top of ERIKA.

11. What do you pay today for software for reports, invoices and time recording?
12. Would you pay €39 a month for what you used? Why / why not?
13. At what price would it be too expensive to consider? At what price would you doubt the quality? (Van Westendorp, two questions only.)
14. Per firm or per technician: which feels fairer for you?
15. What's missing before you'd pay? (DATANORM article import, a specific feature, a trade-specific checklist …)
